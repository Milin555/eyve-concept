Two steps at night, and nothing else. A barrier that has stopped holding is not recovered by adding a fourth product to the evening; it is recovered by the ones already there being the only things asked of it. When the tightness and reactivity have settled, the brightening course is the thing to add — not before.

Most compromised barriers are over-treated rather than under-treated, and the first cause is usually the wash. EYVE’s own line on it: traditional cleansers often contain harsh surfactants that strip natural oils, cause dryness, increase sensitivity, trigger redness, disrupt the skin barrier and leave skin feeling tight. So this routine removes the thing doing the damage first, restores what was lost second, and protects the repair while it happens. Wash, hydrate, protect — in that order, and nothing else in between.

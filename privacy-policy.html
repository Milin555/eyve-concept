Privacy policy

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do not do. It is short because we collect very little.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, Eyve Exports Private Limited, Ring Road, Surat 395002, Gujarat, is the Data Fiduciary for everything described below — the company that decides what is collected and why, and the company answerable for it. If you are reading this as a customer, the Act calls you the Data Principal. It gives you rights, and they are set out further down rather than buried.

What we collect

When you order: your name, delivery address, email and mobile number. We need these to ship to you and to tell you where the parcel is.

When you subscribe: your email address only.

When you contact us: whatever you choose to tell us, including any photographs you send.

We use basic analytics to understand which pages people read. This does not identify you personally.

Why we hold each item

The Act requires a specific purpose for each item rather than a general permission, so here they are, one line each.

Name and delivery address — to print a shipping label and for the courier to find you. Mobile number — so the courier can call before delivery, and so tracking updates reach you. Email address — to send your order confirmation, your invoice and the courier’s tracking link. Order contents and value — to fulfil the order, to handle a return or refund, and to keep the tax records we are required by law to keep. Anything you write to us, including photographs sent with a reaction report — to answer you, and where you have sent a reaction report, to review the formulation concerned. Marketing email address, if you subscribe — to send the monthly note, and nothing else.

We do not use any of it to build a profile of you, to score you, or to make an automated decision about you. There is no such system here.

What we never see

Your card number, CVV, PIN or OTP. Payments are processed entirely by Razorpay. Card details are entered on their systems and never reach ours.

We could not give your card details to anyone even if we were asked, because we do not hold them.

Who we share with

Our courier partner receives your name, address and phone number, because otherwise they cannot deliver.

Razorpay receives what it needs to process payment.

Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade customer data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for seven years, as tax law requires.

Marketing emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Unsubscribing removes you the same day.

Photographs sent for a reaction report are kept only as long as the case is open, unless you give permission to keep them.

Consent, and taking it back

We ask for what an order needs, at the point the order needs it, and we process it on the consent you give then. Marketing is separate and opt-in: not subscribing does not affect an order, and an order does not subscribe you.

You can withdraw consent at any time, and it must be as easy to withdraw as it was to give — one link in any email, or one line to care@eyve.in. When you withdraw, we stop the processing that rested on it and delete what we no longer need. Two honest consequences: withdrawing consent for order data while an order is in transit means we cannot complete that delivery, and withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before it, or erase records tax law requires us to keep.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect the personal data of anyone under 18. The Act requires verifiable parental consent before a child’s data is processed, and it prohibits tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising to children outright — we run none of those on anyone, of any age.

If you believe a child has given us data, write to care@eyve.in and we will delete it and confirm that we have.

If there is a breach

If personal data we hold is lost, exposed or accessed by someone who should not have it, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and every affected person without delay, as the Act requires. The notice will say what happened, what data was involved, what we have done about it and what you should do. We will not wait to establish blame first, and we will not describe it as a ‘technical issue’.

Your rights

Write to care@eyve.in and you can ask for a copy of everything we hold about you and who we shared it with, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it, or withdraw a consent. The Act also lets you nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or cannot act for yourself, and we will record a nomination if you send us one. We act within 30 days, and sooner if we can.

Privacy grievances. If a request is refused, ignored, or handled badly, say so to our Grievance Officer at grievance@eyve.in. The same officer handles privacy complaints and consumer complaints, so there is one route and not two: acknowledgement within 24 hours, resolution within 15 days. The details are on our contact page.

If we still have not resolved it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Exhausting our grievance route first is a condition of that complaint, which is exactly why we publish the timeline we hold ourselves to.

Deletion requests cannot remove order records we are legally required to retain, and we will tell you plainly which records those are.

Cookies

We use a small number: one to remember what is in your bag, and analytics cookies to count page views. There are no advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

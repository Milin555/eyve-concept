The observation

Why the carrier came first

The recurring problem was never a shortage of good actives. It was that so little of each one reached the tissue it was meant to treat.

The brand began from something seen in a clinic: patients arrived using correct ingredients at correct percentages, following sensible routines, and saw modest results.

The formulations were not the problem. What reached the skin was.

EYVE began as an answer to that gap. The delivery system came first. The five formulations were built around it, and there are only five because we could not justify a sixth.

What we will not do

  • 01Claim a result we have not assessed.
  • 02Put a percentage on the front of a bottle and stay quiet about how much of it is usable.
  • 03Tell you that pigmentation clears in two weeks.

Every figure on this site is a specification printed on the pack itself. Where we could not source a number, we removed the claim rather than softened it — which is why no efficacy percentage appears anywhere here.

Made in India, for Indian skin

Formulated and manufactured in India. The sunscreen exists because most SPF 50 on this market leaves a grey cast on deeper skin tones, and that is a reason people stop wearing it. Protection people actually use beats protection that tests well.

The same reasoning runs through the rest of the range. A retinoid nobody can tolerate is not a retinoid anyone benefits from, and a barrier cream that has to be reapplied through the day has not restored a barrier.

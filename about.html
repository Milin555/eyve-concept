Origin

A doctor, a pharmacist,
and a delivery system

The recurring problem was never a shortage of good actives. It was that so little of each one reached the tissue it was meant to treat.

EYVE was founded by Anita Jasani — a doctor and a pharmacist — and co-founded by Devam Jasani, a student at IIT Bombay. The brand began from a clinical observation: patients arrived using correct ingredients at correct percentages, following sensible routines, and saw modest results.

The formulations were not the problem. What reached the skin was.

EYVE began as an answer to that gap. The delivery system came first. The five formulations were built around it, and there are only five because we could not justify a sixth.

What we will not do

We will not claim a result we have not assessed. We will not put a percentage on the front of a bottle and stay quiet about how much of it is usable. We will not tell you that pigmentation clears in two weeks.

Every figure we publish carries the sample size, the duration and the method underneath it. If we cannot source a number, we remove the claim rather than soften it.

Made in India, for Indian skin

Formulated and manufactured in India. The sunscreen exists because most SPF 50 on this market leaves a grey cast on deeper skin tones, and that is a reason people stop wearing it. Protection people actually use beats protection that tests well.

Who makes it

The founders

Anita Jasani

Founder

A doctor and a pharmacist. Every formulation is developed under her direction, and the decision the whole range rests on — encapsulate first, then dose — is hers.

She signs off what goes on a label and what comes off it. Reaction reports reach her, and they are what a reformulation starts from.

Devam Jasani

Co-founder

A student at IIT Bombay. He co-founded EYVE and works on the carrier itself — the vesicle, its size distribution, and whether a batch of it survives the journey it is sold on.

The explaining is his too. If the delivery system cannot be described plainly enough to use correctly, it does not go on a pack.

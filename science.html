What gets there

An active ingredient only works if it arrives where it is needed, at a usable concentration. Most of them do not. This is the part of skincare nobody puts on the front of a bottle.

The delivery chain

Four stages, in the order they happen

EYVE prints this chain on its own cartons and leaflets. It is worth reading slowly, because every claim the brand makes downstream depends on the first two links holding.

01

Liposome

The active is sealed inside a vesicle built from the same class of lipid as your own cell membranes. Sealed, it is also shielded from air and light — which is why an encapsulated active is still an active by the time you use it.

02

Deep penetration

Because the structures match, the barrier admits the vesicle instead of repelling it. In the brand’s own words: Neo-Liposomal actives act deeper at skin-cell level, in their stable form.

03

Active release

The vesicle opens where it arrives and releases gradually, rather than depositing everything at the surface at once. Gradual release is also why tolerance improves at a comparable dose.

04

Visible results

What arrives is what can work. The timeline still belongs to biology: pigment shifts over weeks, collagen over longer. Delivery changes how much of a dose is usable, not how fast skin renews.

What a liposome actually is

A liposome is a microscopic sphere formed from the same class of lipid that makes up your own cell membranes. Because the structures match, the barrier treats an arriving vesicle as something familiar rather than something to repel.

Inside that sphere sits the active. It is shielded from air and light, which is why encapsulated formulations stay stable where free-form actives degrade in the bottle.

Why we say Neo-Liposomal

Conventional liposomes are fragile and often break down before they reach anything useful. Our carrier is engineered for a tighter size distribution and greater stability, so more of each dose survives the journey and releases where it was meant to.

This is why our percentages look modest next to brands competing on concentration. A lower percentage that arrives beats a higher one that does not.

What this does not mean

It does not mean results arrive faster than biology allows. Pigmentation takes eight to twelve weeks to shift. Collagen takes longer. Delivery improves how much of an active is usable — it does not change the timeline of skin renewal, and anyone claiming otherwise is selling something.

In the range

Four jobs, one carrier

The same delivery system is asked to do four different things across the range. Here is what it carries in each formulation, and what the brand actually claims for it.

Photoprotection: three filters, named

LIPOSHIELD™ is SPF 50 PA++++, and that cover comes from three European filters layered together — Bemotrizinol, Uvinul A Plus and Uvinul T Plus — rather than one molecule asked to hold the whole spectrum on its own.

The brand lists superior photostability beside them, and that matters more than the number on the front of the bottle: a filter that breaks down in sunlight has stopped being a filter. Vitamin C and Niacinamide travel in the Neo-Liposomal carrier alongside the filters, which is what the pack calls its protection complex.

Barrier: Ceramide NP, in two places

Ceramides are part of what the barrier is built from, so Ceramide NP is a replacement rather than a coating. It is the active the moisturiser is built around, with Niacinamide, Hyaluronic Acid, Aquaxyl and Centella behind it. The bottle states 24 hours of moisturisation; the listing says 72. We use the bottle’s figure, because where a brand contradicts itself the conservative number is the honest one.

Ceramide NP appears a second time inside Lipobright Pro, with Panthenol, Hyaluronic Acid and Polyglutamic Acid. An active course is only as tolerable as the barrier carrying it, and a brightening serum that damages the barrier undoes itself.

Brightening: MELAZERO® and more than one pathway

Pigment is not produced in a single step, so Lipobright Pro does not answer it with a single active. MELAZERO® leads, with Alpha Arbutin, Niacinamide, PAD, Licorice extract, Vitamin C and Kojic Acid working alongside it. The brand’s own term for this is multi-pathway brightening, and it sells the formula as India’s first Neo-Liposomal brightening serum.

What the carrier contributes is arrival. Ten actives that mostly sit on the surface are not ten actives.

Renewal: Neo-Liposomal HPR

Revilipo Pro is built on Neo-Liposomal HPR — the retinoate the label calls Neo-Liposomal Retinoate — with a Neo-Liposomal Peptide Complex, ceramide barrier support, and Acetyl Glutathione, Astaxanthin and CoQ10.

Retinoids are the class people most often abandon, because the irritation arrives before the result does. Gradual release overnight, into a barrier being supported at the same time, is the brand’s answer to that. It is a tolerance argument, not a speed one.

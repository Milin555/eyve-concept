What a liposome actually is

A liposome is a microscopic sphere formed from the same class of lipid that makes up your own cell membranes. Because the structures match, the barrier treats an arriving vesicle as something familiar rather than something to repel.

Inside that sphere sits the active. It is shielded from air and light, which is why encapsulated formulations stay stable where free-form actives degrade in the bottle.

Why we say Neo-Liposomal

Conventional liposomes are fragile and often break down before they reach anything useful. Our carrier is engineered for a tighter size distribution and greater stability, so more of each dose survives the journey and releases where it was meant to.

This is why our percentages look modest next to brands competing on concentration. A lower percentage that arrives beats a higher one that does not.

What this does not mean

It does not mean results arrive faster than biology allows. Pigmentation takes eight to twelve weeks to shift. Collagen takes longer. Delivery improves how much of an active is usable — it does not change the timeline of skin renewal, and anyone claiming otherwise is selling something.

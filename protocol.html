Why a routine outperforms single products

Actives do not work in isolation. A serum applied to a stripped barrier absorbs unpredictably and irritates more. A brightening course without daily sunscreen loses ground every afternoon. Moisturiser holds an active in contact with the skin for longer.

Each step raises the return on the next. Used alone, any one product works. Used in sequence, each one works harder.

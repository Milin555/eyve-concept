Hydrasyn Ultra-Gentle Cleanser₹799 · 100 ml

EYVE HYDRASYN isn’t a face wash that happens to be gentle. It’s a barrier-first cleansing system engineered to cleanse, hydrate, soothe and protect in a single step — delivering the comfort of a moisturizer with the effectiveness of a cleanser.

EYVE — Hydrasyn product copy

The argument

What a wash normally costs you

A surfactant cleanser

“Traditional cleansers often contain harsh surfactants that strip natural oils, cause dryness, increase sensitivity, trigger redness, disrupt the skin barrier and leave skin feeling tight.”

  • It works by strippingLather is lipid leaving. The dirt goes, and the oil the barrier is built from goes with it.
  • The bill arrives laterTightness first, then dryness, then a barrier that reacts to whatever you put on after it.
  • The next step pays itA serum that stings, and a moisturiser spending its first fortnight repairing the cleanser.

Hydrasyn

A non-surfactant cleansing system. In EYVE’s own words, every wash becomes a treatment step.

  • WashDirt, sebum, make-up and impurities lift off. The skin’s own lipids stay where they are.
  • HydrateThe step leaves water behind in the upper layers rather than taking it away.
  • ProtectAnti-oxidative everyday support, in EYVE’s phrase, out of the same single step.

That is the entire claim, and it is a claim about what is not in the bottle as much as what is. The nine entries below are what is.

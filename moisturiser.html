The argument

Replace the lipid. Don’t seal over it.

A barrier that has lost ceramide does not need a film laid over the top of it. It needs the ceramide back.

Most of what a heavy cream does is occlusion — a layer that slows water leaving, and washes off. Ceramide NP is the lipid a damaged barrier is short of, and the carrier takes it below the surface.

In use

A cream that has to disappear

The finish is the whole argument for a dry-touch moisturiser: it has to be gone by the time sunscreen goes on, or the step after it fails.

Warmed between the palms first, then pressed in rather than rubbed. A pea for the face, a second for the neck — more than that is what leaves a film and makes people conclude the cream is greasy.

The actives

Eight actives, four jobs

A daily moisturiser with Ceramide NP delivered in Neo-Liposomal form to support barrier restoration. Holds hydration for twenty-four hours. The dry-touch finish sits well under sunscreen and make-up, even in humid conditions.

  • Barrier
  • Ceramide NPReplaces the lipid the barrier is missing, rather than sealing over it.
  • Neo-Liposomal carrierCarries the ceramide past the surface instead of onto it.
  • Water
  • Hyaluronic AcidHolds water in the upper layers across the day.
  • AquaxylNamed on the bottle beside the Neo-Liposomal technology, for the twenty-four-hour hold.
  • GlycerinDraws moisture inward and keeps the finish light.
  • Tone
  • NiacinamideThe basis for the brand calling this a brightening moisturiser.
  • Comfort
  • Centella AsiaticaCalms skin that reacts to weather and to actives.
  • PanthenolCalms reactivity while the barrier rebuilds.

Directions

After cleansing, face and neck

  1. 01

    Apply after cleansing, on face and neck, while the skin is still slightly damp.

  2. 02

    Massage a pea-sized amount until it is fully absorbed.

  3. 03

    Twice daily, morning and night. Give it a minute to settle before sunscreen.

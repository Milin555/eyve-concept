Dispatch

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are usually dispatched the same day. Otherwise within 24–48 working hours.

The courier’s own tracking link goes to you by email and on WhatsApp when the parcel leaves us. That link is the live record — there is no account area here and no separate order-status page, and we see exactly what you see. If no link has arrived within two working days, write to us with your order number.

Delivery times

  • Metro cities — typically 2–4 working days from dispatch.
  • Other locations — typically 4–7 working days.
  • Remote PIN codes — longer, and during festival periods everything takes longer. We would rather say so than promise a date we cannot hold.

Shipping charges

  • Free on orders over ₹999.
  • A flat ₹69 below that, anywhere in India.
  • ₹49 handling on Cash on Delivery, which covers what the courier charges us.

Cash on Delivery

Available on most PIN codes for orders up to ₹5,000. You do not have to reach checkout to find out: type your PIN into the delivery check on any product page, in your bag, or on the help page. It returns the working-day window for that PIN, a named delivery date, and whether Cash on Delivery runs on that route. A handful of far routes are prepaid-only, and the check says so before you have entered an address.

Please keep the exact amount ready. Couriers frequently cannot give change.

Repeated refusal of COD parcels may mean we ask for prepayment on future orders. This is not a penalty, it is the cost of returned freight.

If a parcel is late or missing

Check the tracking link first — most delays resolve themselves within 48 hours.

If tracking has not moved in three working days, contact us with your order number. We raise it with the courier and keep you updated rather than asking you to chase them.

If a parcel is confirmed lost, we reship at our cost or refund in full, your choice.

International

We do not ship outside India at present. It is planned, and we would rather not name a date.

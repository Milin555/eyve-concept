The Mechanism

Delivery before dosage

A high concentration on the surface is not the same as a working concentration in the skin. Scroll to follow a vesicle down.

01

The barrier problem

Your skin is built to keep things out. The stratum corneum blocks most topical actives, so much of a conventional formula never gets past the surface.

02

Neo-Liposomal encapsulation

Actives are enclosed in lipid vesicles structurally similar to your own cell membranes. The barrier recognises them, so the payload passes through instead of being turned away.

03

Uniform release

Encapsulation also controls timing. Actives release gradually rather than all at once, which sustains activity and reduces the irritation that comes with sudden exposure.

The Mechanism

Delivery before dosage

01

The barrier problem

Your skin is built to keep things out. The stratum corneum lets small, oil-loving molecules through and turns most actives away at the door.

02

Neo-Liposomal encapsulation

The active travels inside a vesicle built of the same class of lipid as the barrier — so it is recognised rather than resisted.

03

Uniform release

The shell gives way as it descends, so the payload is released gradually instead of all at once at the surface.

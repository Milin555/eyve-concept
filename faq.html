Using the products

Morning: cleanser, moisturiser, sunscreen. Night: cleanser, Lipobright Pro serum, then Revilipo Pro cream. Thinnest texture first, heaviest last. Allow each layer sixty seconds to absorb. Sunscreen is always the final morning step.

Hydration and comfort improve within days. Brightening and pigmentation changes usually become visible between eight and twelve weeks. Firmness and fine-line improvement take twelve weeks or longer. Assess with photographs in consistent lighting, not memory.

Yes. Apply a small amount behind the ear or on the inner forearm for three consecutive days. If there is no reaction, begin facial use. Start the active serum and cream on alternate nights before going daily.

Our cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen are generally considered suitable. For Lipobright Pro and Revilipo Pro, please consult your obstetrician or dermatologist before use. We do not issue blanket clearances for pregnancy. Your doctor knows your history.

Every formulation is developed under a doctor and pharmacist, dermatologically tested, and assessed for tolerance before release. We publish what we test and avoid claims we cannot support.

Speak to your dermatologist first, particularly if you are using retinoids, benzoyl peroxide or hydroquinone. Our formulations are designed to be tolerable alongside most prescriptions, but your prescriber should make that call.

Used as directed, the cleanser lasts about eight weeks, the serum about six, and the moisturiser, sunscreen and night cream about six to eight. Daily sunscreen at the correct quantity will run out fastest, and that is expected.

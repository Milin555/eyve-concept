The argument

Repair is an overnight shift

01

Nothing competes with it

A morning is four products deciding who goes first, and a retinoate is the worst possible neighbour for any of them. At night there is one step, and it has the whole night.

02

The barrier has to survive the course

A renewal active is only worth running if skin holds together while it runs. Ceramide barrier technology is one of the four the pack names, and it is in the same jar for that reason rather than sold back to you as a fifth product.

03

The morning after belongs to the course

Overnight renewal that walks into an unprotected afternoon is renewal spent undoing the afternoon. Sunscreen is not a separate decision from this one.

EYVE’s own positioning for this cream is premium night care — luxury skin repair while you sleep. The pack’s three words for what that is supposed to produce are skin renewal, smoother texture and firm-looking skin, and this page does not go a word further than those three.

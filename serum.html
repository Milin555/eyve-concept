Pigmentation forms through several biological pathways. This serum addresses more than one. MELAZERO, Alpha Arbutin, Niacinamide, PAD and Licorice Extract work together, with Ceramide NP and Panthenol maintaining comfort throughout the course.

  • MELAZEROTargeted melanin control at the synthesis stage.
  • Alpha ArbutinInterrupts tyrosinase activity, the step that drives pigment formation.
  • NiacinamideLimits pigment transfer to surface cells and supports the barrier.
  • PAD (Phenylethyl Resorcinol)Addresses deeper pigmentation that surface actives do not reach.
  • Licorice ExtractCalms the inflammatory response that triggers new pigment.
  • Vitamin CBrightens and supports the other pathways.
  • Kojic AcidA further route to interrupting pigment formation.
  • Centella AsiaticaCalms the skin through an active course.
  • Ceramide NP + PanthenolKeeps the barrier comfortable throughout.
  • Hyaluronic + Polyglutamic AcidHolds hydration so the actives stay tolerable.

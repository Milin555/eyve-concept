The actives

Ten actives, sorted by where they act

Pigmentation forms through several biological pathways. This serum addresses more than one. MELAZERO, Alpha Arbutin, Niacinamide, PAD and Licorice Extract work together, with Ceramide NP and Panthenol maintaining comfort throughout the course.

  • Synthesis — where pigment is made
  • MELAZERO®Targeted melanin control at the synthesis stage.
  • Alpha ArbutinInterrupts tyrosinase activity, the step that drives pigment formation.
  • Kojic AcidA further route to interrupting pigment formation.
  • Vitamin CBrightens, and supports the other pathways as it does.
  • Transfer — where pigment moves
  • NiacinamideLimits pigment transfer to surface cells, and supports the barrier.
  • Depth — where surface actives stop
  • PAD (Phenylethyl Resorcinol)Addresses deeper pigmentation that surface actives do not reach.
  • Inflammation — what triggers new pigment
  • Licorice ExtractCalms the inflammatory response that triggers new pigment.
  • Centella AsiaticaCalms the skin through an active course.
  • Barrier — what makes the course bearable
  • Ceramide NP + PanthenolKeeps the barrier comfortable throughout.
  • Hyaluronic + Polyglutamic AcidHolds hydration so the actives stay tolerable.

Directions

One pump, twice a day

  1. 01

    One pump onto a cleaned face and neck.

  2. 02

    Massage until it is fully absorbed, working over the affected areas first.

  3. 03

    Twice daily, morning and night. Follow with moisturiser; in the morning, finish with sunscreen — without exception.

EYVE’s own note on the formula: “1st drop to last drop, same result.” That is a claim about stability, not about speed — the actives are encapsulated, so the pack is meant to dose the same way on its last day as on its first.

What to expect

Over a course

  1. Week 1

    Skin feels more hydrated. No pigment change yet, and none expected.

  2. Week 4

    Texture and overall dullness begin to improve.

  3. Week 8

    Dark spots typically start to lighten.

  4. Week 12

    Most reported change occurs here. Daily sunscreen holds it.

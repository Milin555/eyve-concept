Returns and refunds

You should be able to try a formulation without risk. This says exactly what we will and will not do.

Last updated 13 September 2026.

We would rather take a product back than have it sit unused. Every clause below is what happens, not what we reserve the right to do.

Cancelling before dispatch

You can cancel any order, for any reason, at any time before it is dispatched. There is no charge and we will not ask why. Email care@eyve.in with your order number, or reply to your confirmation email.

A prepaid order cancelled before dispatch is refunded in full — product price, shipping and the Cash on Delivery handling fee if one was charged. Nothing is withheld. A Cash on Delivery order simply stops: there is nothing to refund, because nothing has been paid.

We dispatch within 24–48 working hours, and same-day for orders placed before 2pm on a working day, so a cancellation needs to reach us quickly. If it arrives after the parcel has left, tell us anyway: you can refuse the parcel at the door, and we will treat a refused, unopened parcel as a cancellation rather than a return, with the return freight at our cost.

If we cancel — stock we do not have, a price we published wrongly, a route the courier will not serve — you are refunded in full and we tell you which of those it was. We will not substitute a product for another one.

Unopened products

Unopened, unused products in their original packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price.

Return shipping on unopened products is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong product, in which case we arrange and pay for collection.

If a product causes a reaction

Stop using it. Contact us with your order number and a photograph of the affected area.

We assess these individually and we do not require the product to be unopened. Where a reaction is consistent with the formulation, we refund in full and we do not ask for the product back.

We also want the report. It goes to our founder, and it informs reformulation.

Damaged or incorrect items

Photograph the parcel and the product and contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We send a replacement at our cost, or refund in full, whichever you prefer. You do not need to return the damaged item.

What we cannot refund

  • Opened products returned because you changed your mind. Cosmetics cannot be resold once the seal is broken, and we will not restock and resell a returned item.
  • Products bought from an unauthorised reseller. We can only stand behind what we shipped.
  • Requests made more than 14 days after delivery — unless a reaction is involved, in which case there is no time limit.

How refunds are issued

  • Prepaid orders — to the original payment method. Razorpay typically returns funds within 5–7 working days, and we have no ability to make that faster.
  • Cash on Delivery orders — by bank transfer. We will ask for account details, and we will never ask for a card number, PIN or OTP.
  • The ₹49 COD handling fee — refunded only where the fault was ours.

How to start a return

Write to care@eyve.in with your order number and the reason. You will get a reply within one working day with a collection slot or a return address.

Please do not send anything back before you hear from us — unannounced returns are difficult to match to an order.
